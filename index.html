About Our School

An Idea-First School Built
for Every Child

Santosh Public School is a caring English Medium primary school in Chhapraula, Greater Noida West, Uttar Pradesh. We provide quality education from Nursery to Class 5, following a child-friendly curriculum that focuses on strong academic foundations and joyful learning.

With dedicated teachers, well-equipped classrooms and activity-based learning, we help children develop confidence, creativity, good manners and essential skills for their future. Our aim is to provide every child with a safe, supportive and encouraging environment where they can learn, grow and succeed.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

What classes are available at Santosh Public School?

We offer classes from Nursery, LKG and UKG through to Class 1 to Class 5 — covering the full primary school curriculum in English medium.

Is transport / bus service available?

Yes, transport facility is available for students. Please contact the school office for route details and bus timings in your area.

What is the medium of instruction?

The medium of instruction is English. We follow a well-structured, child-friendly curriculum designed to build strong academic foundations, essential skills and a lifelong love for learning.

How can I apply for admission?

You can visit the school office directly, call us at +91 99118 26993, or fill out the admission enquiry form on our Admission page.

What is the school timing?

School operates Monday to Saturday from 8:00 AM to 1:30 PM. Sunday is a holiday.

Are co-curricular activities part of the curriculum?

Yes. We include sports, art, music, drawing competitions, annual functions and more as an integral part of every student's school experience.
